# Data sources and scope of reuse

The lunar golden includes the authors' selected no-penumbra geometry and numerical readings of the unadjusted lunar spectrum in Kieffer & Stone (2005, Fig. 8) and of the simulated deployable baffle in Liu et al. (2021, Fig. 15). The two curves are comparison data with their original extraction notes. [Lunar input provenance](data/goldens/lunar/README.md).

This is a local release candidate for the calculations in manuscript A1 v27 r6. The scientific source is the paper's retained fragment model, diffuse sky model, calibrated TESS reduction and supplement S8. Inputs below retain their own origins and terms. An author software license and final confirmation of redistribution terms for the digitized manufacturer curves are outstanding before a public release.

| Input | Origin and preparation |
| --- | --- |
| TESS pixels | Public NASA TESS SPOC calibrated FFI cutout returned by STScI/MAST TESScut (Astrocut 0.11.1 in the retained FITS header). Sector 3, camera 3, CCD 3, 25 × 25 native pixels at ICRS 50.756714°, −60.759542°. Retains the time, flux, quality and WCS extensions. The exact request is recorded in the S3 row of `data/tess/common_visits.csv`. A fresh service response can contain updated headers. The second-field manifest names six SPOC calibrated FFIs by product URI. Acknowledge TESS and MAST, and cite Brasseur et al. (2019) for Astrocut. [MAST data use](https://archive.stsci.edu/publishing/data-use) · [TESScut](https://mast.stsci.edu/tesscut/docs/index.html) · [TESS acknowledgements](https://tess.mit.edu/science-area/getting-started-with-tess/). |
| Stellar inputs and dust | The example stars and unresolved-star photometry derive from ESA Gaia DR3, processed by DPAC. The band-specific, mean-preserving node tables represent the complement at G ≥ 19. The two paper I100 values are samples of the Schlegel, Finkbeiner & Davis 100 μm intensity map. To extend to a new direction, sample the SFD I100 map in MJy/sr there; the E(B−V) reddening map is a different quantity. Diffuse Galactic spectra follow the authors' numerical recovery of Chellew et al. Fig. 4 (CC BY 4.0, extraction information in CSV header) with the published hemisphere factors. [Gaia credits](https://gea.esac.esa.int/archive/documentation/GDR3/Miscellaneous/sec_credit_and_citation_instructions/) · [SFD maps](https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/foreground/fg_sfd_get.html) · [Chellew et al.](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ac6efc). |
| Zodiacal and lunar light | ZodiSURF and the separately stated isotropic residual use the model and Table 4 of O’Brien et al. The TESS cadence-selection operator also carries the STScI tabulated zodiacal background and its colour conversion. The lunar spectral model uses Kieffer & Stone's ROLO constants and the published A7 fallback outside its phase domain. The Liu et al. Fig. 15 simulation supplies the fitted baffle attenuation. Tables carry their source and model conventions. [ZodiSURF](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ae4512) · [Kieffer & Stone](https://doi.org/10.1086/430185) · [Liu et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/rs13132503). |
| Fragment material | Author-fitted diffuse/specular coefficients from Steckel's angular coupon measurements (CaltechDATA, CC0). Aluminium, MLI and solar-cell fits can deviate by more than a factor of two at individual measured angles. BR-127 represents the modeled back surface. The coefficients describe these coupons and the chosen angular model. [Original measurements](https://doi.org/10.22002/m58k4-ct595) · [Steckel & Ehlmann](https://doi.org/10.1029/2025EA004732). |
| Solar and instrument response | Solar irradiance at 1 AU is the authors' 1 nm binning of LASP TSIS-1 HSRS (321–1099 nm). The E5/E12 QE samples are numerical readings from Fig. 11 of ams OSRAM CMV4000 DS000728 v4-00. The optical transmission samples are readings from the optical coating curve on p. 3 of Schneider-Kreuznach TOURMALINE 2.0/35 C, ID 1006219, 12/2021. The paper sampled the three response curves at 5 nm and interpolates them on its 1 nm grid. Manufacturer graphics and datasheet PDFs are not part of this candidate. The digitized numeric traces have no confirmed open redistribution grant. [Solar data](https://lasp.colorado.edu/lisird/data/tsis1_hsrs) · [Coddington et al.](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020GL091709) · [CMV4000 data sheet](https://www.mouser.com/datasheet/2/588/CMV4000_DS000728_4_00-1889788.pdf) · [Lens data sheet](https://schneiderkreuznach.com/download_file/force/3057/3860). |

The two dated sightlines and the hypothetical July lunar position are geometry inputs. The SNR grids, calibration configuration, expectation stamps, initial rotations and tabulated comparison values are the paper authors' simulation outputs. The 57-unit result refers to selected TESS total-background measurements. It does not calibrate the fragment BRDF, individual sky components, E5/E12 responses or lunar baffle.

The historical TESS floor measurements come from the authors' MAST cutout analysis. Each row identifies the original product and retrieval request. The Blender pixels are the authors' linear Cycles renders of specified geometries and shaders. The operational estimator is extracted from Model E's 17 October 2026 source, and the repeated camera-noise experiments come from supplement S8. The angular position covariance example is the Model F shared-state HCW Monte Carlo. The three-read flux covariance experiment was added for this release.
