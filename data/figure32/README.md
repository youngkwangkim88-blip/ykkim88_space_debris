# Fig. 5(a) / Fig. S9(a), first E12 exposure under the August 4 sky

`e12_weak_frame1.npz` contains a lossless extraction from the manuscript supplement's `sequences/E12_weak_date.npz`. The arrays `target_e` (float32), `raw_dn` (uint16), and `calibrated_e` (float32) are frame index 0 of that cube. `sky_rate` (electrons per pixel per second), `distance_km`, and `crop_x` (five integer horizontal offsets in native pixels) are copied from the same archive.

Run `python figure32.py` from the repository root. The example starts from the noiseless source expectation of a rotating grey plate at 329.523 km, integrated for 4 s. The sky rate is 0.7113665 electrons per pixel per second. On the 65 × 1,259 pixel stripe swept across five exposures, seed 8202 generates fixed maps of sensitivity, dark rate and offset, then 64 dark reads and 32 flat reads. The first 65 × 305 crop starts at column 0. A separate seed (88331) generates its raw science frame. Only the target, sky rate and offsets enter those calculations. The stored raw and calibrated images serve as pixel-level checks.

`python figure32.py --output figure32_a.npz` saves the two reconstructed science arrays and the stripe's `master_dark_e` and `flat_minus_dark_e`, both in electrons. For Fig. 5 / Fig. S9 the flat response is `flat_minus_dark_e / 5000`. The program also reports how the answer changes if the measured stripe mean replaces that nominal input. The distance tables propagate uncertainty from full-detector normalization. The stripe covers only part of that detector.

`python figure32.py --from-optics` also integrates the first sampled grey plate's 10 s spin, illumination, spectral response and moving PSF to make a new target expectation. It recomputes the sky rate at each dated sightline. The expected charges for panels (a), (b) and (c) are 1156.69, 1156.69 and 2142.84 e⁻, and their expected SNRs are 11.0638, 9.1170 and 13.7697. For (a), the largest difference from the archived float32 target is 2.37 × 10⁻⁷ e⁻. The newly generated target and sky then drive a pixel-level comparison against the raw and calibrated reference arrays. Panels (b) and (c) have charge and expected SNR comparisons only.
